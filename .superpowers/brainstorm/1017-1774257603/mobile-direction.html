Mobile Animation Direction

Which overall feel should guide the mobile experience?

Slide-up reveals on scroll

A — Editorial Scroll

Each section fades + slides up as it enters view. Clean word-split reveals for headlines. Feels like a luxury magazine scrolling experience. Subtle, premium, no distractions.

Full-screen section transitions

B — Cinematic Swipe

Full-screen sections with horizontal swipe transitions between Hero/About/Work. Dark dramatic feel between sections. Like a premium app onboarding. Bold, immersive, high-impact.

✦

Spring physics + bouncy micro-interactions

C — Springy & Tactile

Spring physics on every interaction — cards bounce in, buttons have press feedback, the profile photo has a parallax float. Feels alive and fun. Great for engagement, slightly less "luxury".
