Intro Animation — Before the Page Loads

Plays once on first visit, then the site reveals. Which style?

📸
0.0s — black screen
0.3s — FLASH ⚡
0.8s — site fades in

A — Camera Flash

Black screen → blinding white flash → site fades in. Exactly like a camera going off. Fast, punchy, ~1s total. The logo appears briefly on the black before the flash.

SHANI
4 panels slide in → close → open → reveal

B — Camera Shutter

4 dark panels slide in from the edges like a camera shutter closing, meet in the center showing the logo — then iris-open to reveal the site. ~1.5s. More dramatic and memorable.
